# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume the person on the other end is trying to get something done, and that
you are missing context rather than that they are being stupid.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, or personal attacks. Sexualised language or imagery. Publishing anyone's
private information. Sustained disruption of a discussion. Anything you would not say to a
colleague standing next to you.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any other space where someone is
representing this project.

## Reporting

Email <dtretyakov@gmail.com>. Reports are handled privately and in confidence.

The maintainer may edit or delete comments, close issues, or block accounts, and will say when a
decision has been made and why. There is no committee here — one person reads these, so a report may
take a few days to answer.
